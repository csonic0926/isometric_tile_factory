# World and character causality — optional method

Use when a world feels decorative or a character cannot generate believable
choices. Inputs: adopted rules, current twin/indexed character packages, scoped
creative assignment, relevant knowledge/event view. No prerequisite step files.

Try a mundane day under the world's material rules. Trace food/work/service,
movement permissions, reporting lines and objects changing hands. Name what
breaks the ordinary rhythm, who notices and what enforcement or cost follows.
An exception should reveal the rule's boundary, not erase it.

For a character, put role, need and visible contradiction under one ordinary
pressure. Ask what the person does before explaining what they are like. Let
habits, avoidance and speech emerge from that pressure and social position;
contrast the closest existing character's function rather than adding labels.
Test the voice in a concrete exchange. Check the speaker's actual evidence,
relationships and forbidden knowledge, not the author's omniscient understanding.

Write the final character package to CHARACTER_SCHEMA.md with index references;
world content goes to the existing twin's canonical seeds/facts/relations. Do not
create conceptual/world-role/behavior handoffs just to feed another method.
Unknown details remain scoped assumptions or open questions. Final prose and
world/character quality still obey the mandatory Story capability contract.
