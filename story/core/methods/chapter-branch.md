# Chapter and branch causality — optional method

Use for an inert story line, weak emotional progression, knowledge leak or a
cosmetic branch. Inputs: source commission/ruled beats, adopted canon and voice,
legitimate branch/time state; runtime capabilities only when writing for a game.

Find an ordinary track, the pressure that bends it, and why this time window
matters. Each segment should change what someone can do, know, risk or feel.
Keep concrete scene prose alongside causal links; do not write a chain of
summaries and expect a later worker to invent the experience.

If assigned beats exist, test the complete curve against the original ruled
sheet, not a paraphrased delivery plan. Hold before releasing; avoid dialogue
explaining the intended emotion. Fit the timeframe to the story, not a default
number of scenes/days. Surface missing engineering without blocking pure prose.

For a branch, pin the trunk point, inherited knowledge and first divergence.
Ask what the actor physically does now, who witnesses it, what changes later,
and why the alternate route cannot be identical. Distinguish world truth from
what each person learned. Test shared nodes from both histories. Preserve the
mandatory branch consequences/delayed hook and output exact changed knowledge,
relations and follow-up possibilities rather than repeated handoff restrictions.

For game delivery, inspect actual visual grammar and route primitives; verify
changed moments rather than copying trunk staging. Later technical packaging
must not add choices or delete emotional time from the reviewed work.
